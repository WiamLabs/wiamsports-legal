1. Who we are

WiamLabs (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates WiamArena, a football intelligence product delivered on Telegram. We are based in Ghana.

Data controller: WiamLabs

Telegram is a separate company. Your Telegram account is governed by Telegram’s Privacy Policy. We only receive the data Telegram sends to our bot when you interact with us.

2. What WiamArena is

WiamArena is not a public fan app inside the bot chat. It has three surfaces:

  • Free Channel — match winner and confidence. May be complimentary or a paid monthly Telegram Stars subscription. WiamLabs can change that.
  • VIP Channel — full cards. Usually a paid monthly Telegram Stars subscription. WiamLabs may run complimentary periods, then return to paid access. The Stars price can go up or down.
  • Affiliate — promoters share tracked join links and are paid in prediction cards, not cash.

The bot chat with @WiamSportsBot is a private workspace for the WiamLabs sports team. Unauthorised visitors are directed to the channels and, if they choose, the affiliate desk. We are not a bookmaker. We do not accept bets, hold stakes, or pay winnings.

3. Who this policy covers

It applies when you:

  • Open or message @WiamSportsBot
  • Join or view the Free Channel or VIP Channel
  • Pay for channel access through Telegram Stars (Free Channel when it is paid, and VIP)
  • Join the affiliate programme or join a channel through a promoter’s link
  • Read these legal pages on our website

It does not cover bookmakers, other Telegram bots, or websites you visit on your own.

  • Deliver the bot, channels, and affiliate desk
  • Attribute Free and VIP joins to the correct promoter and send the cards we owe
  • Confirm VIP access after Telegram processes Stars
  • Moderate spam in channels where the bot is an administrator
  • Protect the private team workspace from unauthorised use
  • Improve prediction quality using match results, not your private messages
  • Comply with law and investigate abuse

We do not sell personal data.

Where GDPR / UK GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on:

  • Contract — to provide the service you request (bot replies, channel access, affiliate desk)
  • Legitimate interests — security, fraud prevention, attribution of affiliate joins, product improvement on match data
  • Consent — where we ask you to confirm age or accept terms
  • Legal obligation — if a lawful request requires us to keep or disclose records

In Ghana we also process personal data in line with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

7. Telegram Stars and payments

When a channel is in paid mode, access is billed as a Telegram Stars subscription. Telegram (and Apple or Google if you pay through their in-app systems) is the payment processor. We receive a confirmation that you subscribed or cancelled. We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers.

WiamLabs may offer a channel as complimentary (open join, no monthly Stars fee). Complimentary access is not a promise that the channel stays free. When we switch a channel to paid mode, new joins require payment at the then-current Stars price shown in Telegram. Unpaid access can end at the close of the period.

The Stars price for each channel can increase or decrease. The price that applies is the one Telegram shows you before you pay or renew — not an old screenshot or an old link.

Refunds, chargebacks, and Stars balances are handled under Telegram’s rules and the platform you paid on. Contact Telegram or your app store for payment disputes; contact us if access did not unlock after a successful payment.

8. Affiliate tracking

If you become a promoter, we create a unique tracked invite pair each Ghana day for the Free Channel and the VIP Channel. We store:

  • Your Telegram ID as promoter
  • The token and invite URL for that day’s window
  • The Telegram ID of a person who joined through your link
  • Cards owed and cards we later send you

Pay is prediction cards in your bot chat, not cash, bank transfer, or Stars. You cannot credit your own join. WiamLabs team accounts do not earn. We do not publish other promoters’ names on the public leaderboard beyond what the desk already shows you.

People who join through your link are told by Telegram that they are joining a channel or paying Telegram. We do not email them on your behalf.

9. Channels and moderation

Each channel may be complimentary or paid. When complimentary, posts may still be public or member-only depending on Telegram settings. When paid, membership is controlled by Telegram Stars and by us as channel administrator. We may create unique invite links, moderate spam, and remove members who have not paid after a complimentary period ends.

11. How long we keep it

  • Bot / team accounts — while you use the service, then as needed for security and disputes
  • Affiliate ledger — while cards remain owed, and afterwards for a reasonable period to prevent double-pay and fraud
  • Channel join tokens — tied to daily windows; unused links can be revoked
  • Match predictions and results — kept to operate Track Record and improve the engine; this is not your betting history
  • Website logs — typically 30–90 days unless needed for an investigation

You may ask us to delete personal account data. See Section 13. We may retain limited records where we must (fraud, legal claims, unpaid affiliate debt until settled).

12. Security

  • HTTPS / TLS for this website and database connections
  • Secrets stored in the host environment, not in public pages
  • Team workspace limited to Telegram IDs granted by WiamLabs
  • Rate limits and abuse blocks

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data we will notify you and regulators where the law requires it.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct it
  • Delete it (except where we must keep a record)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Receive a portable copy
  • Lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Ghana, the Data Protection Commission)

Email privacy@wiamlabs.com from a mailbox we can match to you, or message @WiamSportsBot from the same Telegram account. Include your Telegram numeric ID if you know it. We aim to respond within 30 days.

14. Age restriction

WiamArena is for adults 18 years or older (or the higher legal age for gambling-related content where you live). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If a minor has used the service, contact us and we will close the account.

Where data leaves Ghana or your home country, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer (including standard contractual clauses where they apply).
